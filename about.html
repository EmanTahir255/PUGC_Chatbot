About "PUGC SmartBot"

PUGC SmartBot is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to simplify campus life for students, staff, and visitors of Punjab University Gujranwala Campus. It provides instant access to information about courses, events, admissions, and facilities.

Our Mission

To provide Punjab University Gujranwala Campus with a unified, AI-driven digital ecosystem that simplifies academic inquiries, streamlines administrative processes, and enhances the student experience through innovative technology.

How It Works

PUGC SmartBot leverages advanced AI and Natural Language Processing to serve as your personal campus guide. Simply log in, ask your questions about admissions, programs, or campus life, and get instant, accurate responses. Unlock our Premium tier for advanced services like digital application forms, fee challan generation, and full chat history access.

Key Features

AI-Powered Academic Assistant

Get instant, accurate answers for admissions, program details, and general campus queries 24/7.

Digital Application Portal

Access and submit official university application forms directly through your student dashboard.

Fee & Challan Management

Generate official fee challans and upload payment receipts for quick administrative verification.

Premium Benefits

Unlock exclusive tools including real-time event updates, detailed campus resources, and saved chat history.
